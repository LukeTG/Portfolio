Hula Dance Hoop

The Hula Dance Hoop is a is a devising of my own that I conceived by combining Friday Night Funkin' and the Spark Lab Makey Makey Hula Hoop Control Pad. At first it was four cardboard buttons that I made with springs, but then I got the idea to just make the conductivity of your foot complete the circuit, and finally I combined that idea with the hula hoop and made this device.'

Wire Breaking Alarm System

I got this idea from a problem board in the Makey Makey unit. I immiedetly knew what to do and made a alarm system with a breakable wire and a circuit on the ground. There was not much issue making this besides finding out how to put it up on the wall. I also used scratch to code the program that played the alarm sound.
